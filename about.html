About

Learning architecture with operational depth.

My work sits at the intersection of Learning & Development, learning systems, curriculum design, knowledge management, program leadership, data, and technology. Across more than a decade of professional work, I have focused on making complex learning environments clearer, more repeatable, and easier to operate.

Professional focus

I have worked across corporate L&D, higher education, digital learning, curriculum architecture, work-integrated learning, and technology-enabled field programs. The common thread is systems design: understanding how people, content, technology, governance, evidence, and operational constraints interact.

At Suitmedia, I worked on learning architecture and an internal LMS ecosystem. At Institut Teknologi Del, my work included curriculum redesign, academic-program leadership, internship systems, laboratory learning, accreditation coordination, and course design. I have also worked on digital-health implementation where user behavior, data quality, and software performance had to be addressed together.

My current professional direction increasingly connects this background with AI-enabled learning, evaluation, analytics, and decision support. I am particularly interested in systems where learning recommendations, evidence, and human judgment need to work together responsibly.
